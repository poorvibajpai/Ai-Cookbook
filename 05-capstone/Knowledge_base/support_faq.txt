[Account Access]
Q: I forgot my password. How can I reset it?
A: Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email address.

Q: I did not receive the password reset email. What should I do?
A: Check your spam or promotions folder first. If it is not there, wait 5 minutes and try again. If the issue continues, contact support.

[Billing]
Q: Can I get a refund?
A: Refunds are available within 7 days of purchase for eligible plans. Refund requests must include the account email and reason for the request.

Q: How long do refunds take?
A: Approved refunds usually appear in the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

Q: Can I change my subscription plan?
A: Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your subscription from the billing section of your account settings.

[Orders]
Q: How do I check my order status?
A: You can check your order or subscription status from your account dashboard under "Orders" or "Billing".

Q: My payment failed. What should I do?
A: Confirm your card details, available balance, and bank restrictions. Then retry the payment or use a different payment method.

[Technical Support]
Q: The app is slow or not loading. What should I do?
A: Refresh the page, clear browser cache, and try again. If the problem continues, test another browser or device.

Q: The feature I need is not working. What should I include in my support message?
A: Include the feature name, what you expected, what happened instead, and any screenshot or error message.

[Escalation]
Q: When should a case be escalated to human support?
A: Escalate when the user reports repeated payment failure, account lockout, security concerns, data loss, or urgent business impact.

Q: What is a high-priority complaint?
A: Messages that mention being extremely frustrated, angry, charged incorrectly multiple times, or unable to access a paid account should be treated as high priority.

[Tone Guidance]
Q: How should support replies sound?
A: Support replies should be calm, clear, empathetic, and action-oriented. Avoid blaming the user.
